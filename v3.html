The booking-fee problem

You're paying a commission on your own reputation.

Most Quandoo bookings aren't strangers browsing for dinner — they're people who Googled your restaurant by name and happened to click the widget. You pay €2–4 every time a regular sits down. That's up to €200 a year for one loyal guest.

And the guest data — visits, preferences, contact details — lives on their servers, not yours. They use it to market other restaurants to your own customers.
