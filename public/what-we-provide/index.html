What we provide

Everything your restaurant runs on, from one place.

Klar builds and runs the digital side of independent restaurants in Finland: your own website, your own online ordering and your own table bookings, with the guest list staying yours. One partner, one monthly price, and we set all of it up for you.

  • Your website Built for your restaurant, not a template. Found on Google and by the AI assistants people now ask for dinner. Menu or hours change, you message us, it is live the same day.
  • Bookings On your own site, in your own design. Confirmations, reminders that stop no-shows, waitlist, floor plan, one screen for the whole team.
  • Ordering Your menu, your site, 0% commission. Eat in or take away, paid at the restaurant. Orders land on a screen with an alert for the kitchen.
  • Your guest list Every booking and order builds your own CRM. Visit history, allergies, birthdays. Download it any time. Leave and you take it with you.

  • Same-day content changes, as often as you like
  • Your existing domain connected for you
  • Review requests after every visit and order
  • A plain monthly report: bookings, orders, where people came from

Do I have to leave Wolt and Foodora?
No, and we'd tell you not to. They're good at bringing you people who've never heard of you. They're expensive for the customer who already orders from you every Thursday. Keep the first, move the second.
Who owns the guest list after the move?
You do. Guests, order history, contact details, it's yours, it's exportable, and if you ever leave you take it with you. The website is yours as well, not rented from us: if you leave, we hand the whole thing over, site, domain and data, and you carry on with it somewhere else.
