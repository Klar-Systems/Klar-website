Questions about the partner price

What does a Klar partner pay each month?
Nothing for the first six months, counted from the day your site goes live. From month seven it is €199 a month + VAT, locked. Klar 300, Klar 750 or Klar 1500 — it does not matter. You pay €199. It does not rise when you grow, and it does not rise when our prices do.
How much is the setup fee as a partner?
40% off the published setup fee: quoted €2,250 + VAT, you pay €1,350 + VAT. You choose how to pay it — 50% before work starts and 50% before launch, or spread across up to three monthly instalments. The total is the same either way, with no interest and no handling charge.
Can I take only bookings or only ordering at the partner price?
The partner price covers the full package: website, online ordering and the booking system together. Take one part on its own and it is the list price for that part.
Is this a limited offer?
No. These are Klar's standard terms for every restaurant taking the whole system — no deadline, no limited number of places, and no price that goes up once some number of restaurants have signed. The minimum term is 12 months, and the six free months are counted inside those 12.
