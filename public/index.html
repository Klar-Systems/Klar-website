The Problem

Three companies stand between you and your own customers.

The delivery app takes up to 30% of every order. On a €30 order that is €9 gone before you have paid for the food, and most of those orders are regulars who already knew your name.

The booking platform charges you €2–4 every time a guest sits down. A regular who books every Friday costs you up to €200 a year, for a customer you already had.

And the guest list that all of this creates, the names, the numbers, the order history, sits on their servers. They use it to put the restaurant down the street in front of the people who eat at yours.

You built the reputation. They rent it back to you.
