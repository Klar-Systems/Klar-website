The Commission Problem

You built the reputation. The delivery app charges you to use it.

On a €30 order at 30% commission, roughly €9 leaves your till before you've paid for the food. Two hundred orders a month is €1,800 — €21,600 a year, gone, from customers who mostly already knew your name before they opened the app.

The regulars are the painful part. Someone who orders from you every week isn't discovering you on Wolt. They're using Wolt as a remote control. You pay a finder's fee on a customer nobody had to find.

And that customer isn't yours. Their name, their order history, their phone number — all of it sits on the platform's servers, and it gets used to show them the restaurant next door.

The Booking Fee Problem

Your booking system charges you every time a customer sits down.

A regular who books through Quandoo every Friday? You pay €2–4 per visit for a customer who already knows and loves your restaurant. That's up to €200 a year for one loyal guest. Multiply that across all your regulars.

And the guest data — visit history, preferences, contact details? That lives on Quandoo's servers, not yours. They use it to promote other restaurants to your own customers. You're building someone else's business with every booking.

Most Quandoo bookings aren't people browsing quandoo.fi for dinner. They're people who Googled your restaurant by name and happened to book through Quandoo's widget. You're paying a commission on your own reputation.

Your customers

The guest list is yours. That is the part nobody tells you.

Every guest who books a table or orders food goes into your own customer list — their name, their phone number, what they ordered, when they last came in, what they're allergic to, when their birthday is. Over a year that list becomes the most valuable thing your restaurant owns.

On the platforms, that list is theirs

Wolt, Foodora and Quandoo keep your guests on their servers. You get an order, not a customer. And they use that same list to put the restaurant down the street in front of the people who eat at yours.

With Klar, it sits in your account

You can open it, search it, and download the whole thing to a spreadsheet whenever you feel like it. You don't have to ask us, and you don't have to explain why.

If you leave, you take it with you

Your guest list, your domain, your photos, your menu, your text. You walk out with all of it. We don't hold your customers hostage to keep you paying — if we're worth the money we'll earn it every month.

And it works for you while it sits there

Quiet Tuesday coming? We can write to the regulars who haven't been in for a while, or the ones with a birthday this week — personally, in their language, from what the list already knows. Try doing that with a delivery app.

Put plainly: you are not renting your customers from us. They were always yours — we just stop other companies from standing between you and them.
